Project 01 / Case Study
The Project

A clean finished bathroom starts with what you never see.

This project required a complete wet-area waterproofing and sealing solution designed to protect the bathroom structure while maintaining a clean, precise finish around critical joints and transitions.

The focus was not simply on visual presentation. The real work was in preparation, substrate assessment, moisture-sensitive detailing, sealing continuity and ensuring each stage supported long-term durability.

The Challenge

Protecting the room before perfecting the finish.

Bathrooms combine water exposure, movement, multiple materials and tight junctions. That means small installation details can have a significant impact on the future performance of the room.

01

Critical Junctions

Wall-to-floor transitions and penetrations required careful preparation and continuous sealing.

02

Surface Preparation

The substrate needed to be clean, stable and correctly prepared before waterproofing could begin.

03

Finish Integration

Waterproofing and sealing had to support the final tile and fixture detailing without creating visible disruption.

Project Details

Precision in every layer.

The project combined technical preparation with clean finishing so the completed bathroom remained visually minimal while the protection behind the surface stayed continuous.

Our Solution

A methodical waterproofing system, delivered step by step.

01

Assessment & Preparation

The wet area was reviewed for surface condition, transitions, penetrations and any locations where movement or moisture could compromise performance.

02

Joint Reinforcement

Critical junctions were prepared and reinforced so the waterproofing system could remain continuous across changes in plane and material.

03

Waterproofing Application

The membrane system was applied with controlled coverage and attention to required wet-area zones, edges and penetrations.

04

Final Sealing & Detail

Once the finished surfaces were installed, final sealing focused on clean lines, water-sensitive joints and a finish that visually disappeared into the completed room.

The Result

A finished space designed to stay finished.

The completed bathroom achieved the clean visual result expected from a contemporary interior while maintaining the waterproofing and sealing details required behind the surface.

For Seal A Gap, that balance is the point: work should look refined when complete, but its real value is how reliably it continues to perform over time.

“Good waterproofing should become invisible once the room is finished.”
