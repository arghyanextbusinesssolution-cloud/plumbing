Who We Are

Practical expertise for properties that need to perform as well as they look.

From protecting wet areas and sealing building joints to maintaining commercial properties and completing difficult-access work, Seal A Gap approaches every project with the same mindset: understand the problem, choose the right solution and deliver the work properly.

Our experience across construction and property maintenance allows us to look beyond the immediate repair. We consider the surrounding building, access requirements, materials, durability and finish so the completed work makes sense as part of the whole property.
